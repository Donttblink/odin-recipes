Donttblink's Meatballs

Description

An authentic italian seasoned meatball that you can use for sauces, slow cook or even for sandwhiches

This can be made with all beef, beef and pork, veal and pork, or any kind of mince you like!

Ingredients

  • 900g Mince (I like half veal half pork)
  • 1/4 cup of milk
  • 1 cup bread crumbs
  • 2 tsp of salt
  • 1 tsp pepper
  • 2 whole eggs
  • 1/4 cup grana padano cheese (finely grated)
  • 1/2 tsp oregano

Steps

  1. Place mince in a large bowl and let rest to room temperature before adding ingredients
  2. Add half of the total seasoning and slowly start to combine.
  3. Add 2 eggs, bread crumbs, milk and cheese to mixture and continue to mix slowly
  4. Add second half of seasoning blend and mix just enough to combine everything easily and roll into large ball. Do Not Over Mix the Mince! This will lead to a very firm dense meatball
  5. Make a small patty and fry in oil in a pan to test seasoning mixture and adjust.
  6. To cook start by frying meatballs in a large sauce pan until there is some browing on both sides.
  7. To finish place seared meatballs in sauce of choice, my favourite is a tomato sauce, and submerge and let cook for a minimum of 30 minutes.
