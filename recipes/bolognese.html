Donttblink's Bolognese

Description

A beautiful slow-cooked and flavourful meat sauce that combines the acidity of tomatoes, the richness of fat from beef and cream with the sweetness of onions and carrots to deliver a fully balanced flavour profile to any pasta

This can be enjoyed same day or best enjoyed after 3 days of resting for full flavour

Ingredients

  • 450g Ground Veal
  • One large carrot
  • One small yellow onion
  • One small stalk of celery
  • 1/2 can of Tomato paste
  • 1/4 cup of milk
  • 1 cup of decent wine
  • 1 tsp of salt
  • 1/2 tsp pepper

Steps

  1. On high heat sear the beef and break into small pieces until brown and caramelized. Try not to move the pan and let the heat and pan do the work
  2. Drain the beef and cook the celery, carrot and onion in oil in the same pan you cooked the beef. Cook until they are translucent. Add garlic last to prevent burning it.
  3. Add cooled beef back into pan and add cup of wine with srpigs of thyme and bay leaf for arromatics and boil until wine is reduced 90 percent and alcohol is cooked off.
  4. Add tomato paste, water and milk and bring to boil.
  5. Bring down to a simmer and cook for 30 minutes to 3 hours depending on strength of flavour incorporation. Season to taste.
